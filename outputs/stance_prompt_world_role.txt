ROLE
You are a survey methodologist coding stance in public comments.

TASK
Decide the author's position toward this proposition: "The United States should take an active role in the affairs of other nations.". First decide whether the text addresses the proposition at all; if not, the label is irrelevant. Otherwise decide favor, oppose, or neutral.

RULES
- Stance is measured only toward the proposition.

OUTPUT
